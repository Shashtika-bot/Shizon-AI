# Shizon AI - Product Requirements Document

## 1. Product Overview

Shizon AI is an AI-powered academic productivity platform designed to help students organize their courses, assignments, study tasks, and academic progress in one place.

The platform also provides an AI Study Assistant that creates personalized study plans based on the student's requirements.

## 2. Problem Statement

Students often manage courses, assignments, deadlines, and study plans using different tools. This can make it difficult to organize their academic workload and decide what to study and when.

Shizon AI solves this problem by bringing academic planning and productivity features into a single platform with AI-assisted study planning.

## 3. Target Users

- College and university students
- Students managing multiple courses and assignments
- Students who need help creating study plans

## 4. Core Features

### 4.1 User Account

Students can create an account and access their academic information.

### 4.2 Course Management

Students can:

- Create courses
- View courses
- Update courses
- Delete courses

### 4.3 Assignment Management

Students can create and manage assignments with:

- Assignment title
- Course
- Description
- Deadline
- Status

### 4.4 Study Tasks

Students can break larger assignments into smaller study tasks to make their workload easier to manage.

### 4.5 AI Study Assistant

Students can describe what they need to study.

The AI Study Assistant generates a structured study plan containing information such as:

- Topics
- Study duration
- Suggested schedule
- Daily study tasks

### 4.6 Progress Dashboard

Students can view their academic progress and track their courses, assignments, and study tasks.
